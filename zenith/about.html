OUR STORY · SINCE 2019

FROM TECHNOLOGY
TO TRANSFORMATION.

Zenith opened in 2019 with a simple objective: create a serious training space where people could do more than just show up and use equipment.

Founder and coach Anup came from software development. That mindset still shapes Zenith — structure, systems, measurement and continuous improvement instead of guesswork.
