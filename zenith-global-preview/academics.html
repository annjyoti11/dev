01 · FOUNDATIONAL YEARS

Small beginnings. Big discoveries.

A welcoming introduction to learning through play, expression and everyday discovery.

  • Language and communication
  • Early numeracy and problem-solving
  • Creative expression and learning together

02 · MIDDLE SCHOOL

New questions. Wider horizons.

A time to connect ideas, develop independent thinking and discover new interests.

  • Understanding across subjects
  • Reasoning and collaboration
  • Confidence and responsibility

03 · SENIOR SECONDARY

Greater focus. New possibilities.

Deeper learning and greater independence, with thoughtful preparation for the next stage of life.

  • Focused subject exploration
  • Independent study and analytical thinking
  • Conversations about future learning pathways
