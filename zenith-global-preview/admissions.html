A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Your questions
matter.

Which class should my child apply for?

Discuss your child’s age, previous schooling and current learning stage with the admissions team. They will confirm the appropriate class and entry requirements.

Can we visit the school first?

Include a campus visit request in your enquiry. A date and time must be confirmed by the school.

Where can I find fees and required documents?

The approved fee structure and document checklist have not yet been supplied for this preview. Request the current details from the school before applying.

Does this preview submit an application?

No. The enquiry tool prepares a downloadable note only. It does not send information or reserve a place.
