BEGINNER
FOUNDATION.

Three full-body sessions each week. Learn the movements, leave a little in reserve, and build consistency before adding more.

SESSION 02 · BFULL BODY

Move with control.

  1. Easy cardioWarm-up · 5–7 minEasy
  2. Smith machine squat to benchCoach to set the bar and safeties2 × 8–10
  3. Smith machine bench pressAsk a coach to set the safeties2 × 8–10
  4. Cable lat pulldownCoach to set the station2 × 8–12
  5. Bench-supported glute bridgePause gently at the top2 × 10–12
  6. Comfortable cardioOptional finish8–10 min
+

First time on a station? Ask a Zenith coach to adjust the machine, show you the movement and check your setup. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Choose a load that leaves about 2–3 good reps in reserve.

HOW TO PROGRESS

When you can complete the top of the rep range with steady form in both sets, add the smallest available weight next time. If your form changes, keep the same load.
